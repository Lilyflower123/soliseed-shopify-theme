Brand:
Soliseed is a grow light brand focused on helping plants grow where they are placed.

Main slogan:
Every Spot Can Grow.

Core message:
Flexible grow lights for shelves, desks, corners, and indoor growing spaces.

Strategic positioning:
- Amazon is the primary sales and conversion channel.
- The independent website supports brand trust, product selection, setup education, warranty registration, customer retention, and long-term SEO.
- The site should guide users by growing spot and setup form.

Do:
- Focus on growing spot + setup.
- Use clear language for non-expert plant owners.
- Keep all sections editable in Shopify theme editor.
- Use reusable snippets and sections.
- Use product metafields for Amazon URLs when possible.
- Prioritize mobile-first layouts.
- Preserve current products and collections unless instructed.
- Make Buy on Amazon the main purchase CTA.
- Add Setup Guide and Warranty Registration as secondary CTAs.

Do not:
- Do not edit main.
- Do not position Soliseed as a generic home lighting brand.
- Do not position Soliseed as a complex plant-specific light recipe brand.
- Do not directly edit the live production theme.
- Do not remove existing products.
- Do not hard-code Amazon links if metafields can be used.
- Do not add review-incentive language.
- Do not promise unrealistic plant growth results.
- Do not expose empty collections.
- Do not leave placeholder text.
- Do not create a Shopify theme from scratch unless explicitly asked.

Key pages:
- Homepage
- Product pages
- Shop by Space pages
- Shop by Setup pages
- Find My Setup
- Setup Guide
- Warranty Registration
- FAQ
- Contact

Primary CTA:
Buy on Amazon

Secondary CTAs:
Find My Setup
View Setup Guide
Register Warranty

Tone:
Clear, useful, calm, plant-focused, setup-focused.
Avoid overly technical language unless in Light Science or FAQ sections.
